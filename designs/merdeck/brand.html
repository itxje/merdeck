Merdeck brand mark

Merdeck reads as Mermaid plus deck. Three flowchart nodes and their edges form an M standing on a deck bar. The mark is stroke-only in currentColor, drawn on a 0 0 24 24 viewBox at stroke width 1.7 with round caps and joins, so it drops into the same sizing and theming as the icon set already used in the product. Approved by the project owner on 2026-09-09.

Alternate directions — for review only

Alternate A — solid nodes

Alternate · not chosen

Keeps the wave line beneath the deck and fills the three nodes solid. Holds more weight at 16 px, but reads heavier than the stroke-only icon set already in the header and loses the open-node flowchart reading at large sizes.

Alternate B — deck with wave

Alternate · not chosen

The former primary direction: an extra wave line beneath the deck carries the Mermaid half of the name directly, but at small sizes the added band around the nodes can read as a crown rather than a flowchart. That reading is why the deck-without-wave direction was chosen instead.

Status: mark approved, layout still under review. The project owner reviewed this sheet and chose the deck-without-wave direction as the product mark on 2026-09-09. Its visual defaults — stroke weight, node radius, spacing and both surface treatments — were chosen from the design tokens already in this project (the shadcn base-nova palette and radius scale in web/src/index.css, mirrored by designs/merdeck/style.css).

No colour is hardcoded: the mark inherits currentColor, so the header tile renders it in --primary-foreground on --primary, and standalone placements follow --foreground in both themes. The two alternates above were reviewed and not chosen; they are kept here so the direction can be compared rather than assumed. The prototype layout as a whole — density, hierarchy and every surface besides the mark itself — remains a proposal and is still under review.
